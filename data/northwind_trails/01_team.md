# Northwind Trails: the team

Northwind Trails is a small company that builds a hiking app called TrailMate.

Maya Chen is the CTO of Northwind Trails. She leads the Platform team and reports to the CEO, Daniel Okafor.

The Platform team owns the TrailMate backend, the offline maps service and the data pipeline.
Ravi Patel is a backend engineer on the Platform team. He maintains the offline maps service.
Lena Fischer is a data engineer on the Platform team. She built the data pipeline that imports
trail conditions from park rangers every night.

The Mobile team builds the TrailMate iOS and Android apps. Sofia Alvarez leads the Mobile team.
Tom Becker is a mobile engineer who works on offline mode in the Android app.
