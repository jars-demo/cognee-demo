# Meridian Space Lab: people

Meridian Space Lab is a small research lab that builds satellites to study Earth.

Dr. Amara Okonkwo is the director of Meridian Space Lab.

The Instruments team designs and calibrates the sensors that fly on the lab's satellites.
Kenji Watanabe leads the Instruments team. Priya Raman is a data scientist on the Instruments
team; she calibrates the HALO-2 spectrometer.

The Mission Operations team plans launches and flies the satellites once they are in orbit.
Elena Petrova leads Mission Operations. Samuel Adeyemi is a flight controller on her team.
