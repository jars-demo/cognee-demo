# Meridian Space Lab: missions

## Mission Lumen

Mission Lumen is a small satellite that studies the upper atmosphere. It carries the HALO-2
spectrometer, built by the Instruments team. Elena Petrova is the mission lead. Lumen is
scheduled to launch in the autumn window.

## Mission Tidewatch

Mission Tidewatch will survey ocean colour to track algae blooms along the coast. It carries the
AQUA-CAM camera, built by the partner company Coastline Optics. Kenji Watanabe is the mission
lead. Tidewatch is delayed until AQUA-CAM passes its thermal vacuum tests.
