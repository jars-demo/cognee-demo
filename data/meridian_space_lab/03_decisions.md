# Meridian Space Lab: decisions log

- The lab decided to delay Mission Tidewatch by three months, because AQUA-CAM failed its first
  thermal vacuum test. Coastline Optics is redesigning the camera's heat shield. Dr. Amara
  Okonkwo approved the delay.
- Mission Operations decided to share a ground station with the Northern Polar Network instead of
  building its own, to save money. Elena Petrova proposed it.
- The Instruments team decided to recalibrate HALO-2 every month in orbit, because its readings
  drift as the sensor ages. Priya Raman owns the calibration schedule.
