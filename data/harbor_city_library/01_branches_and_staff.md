# Harbor City Library: branches and staff

Harbor City Library is a public library with three branches: Central, Eastside and Pier.

Grace Liu is the head librarian. She runs the Central branch, which holds the local history
collection.

Marcus Bell manages the Eastside branch. Eastside has the library's maker space, with 3D printers
and a sewing corner.

Nadia Haddad coordinates the volunteers. She works from the Pier branch, which is the smallest
and opens only in the afternoons.
