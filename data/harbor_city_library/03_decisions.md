# Harbor City Library: decisions log

- The library started lending laptops, because a survey showed that a third of Code Club members
  had no computer at home. Marcus Bell proposed the program; Grace Liu approved the budget.
- Code Club moved from Central to Eastside, so it could use the maker space next door.
- The Pier branch kept its afternoon-only hours, because most of its visitors are families who
  come after school. Nadia Haddad asked the volunteers before the decision was made.
