# Harbor City Library: programs

## Code Club

Code Club teaches teenagers to program on Saturday mornings at the Eastside branch. It is run by
Omar Diallo, a volunteer recruited by Nadia Haddad. The club uses the laptops from the library's
lending program.

## Story Time

Story Time is a reading session for young children every Wednesday at the Pier branch. Volunteers
take turns reading; Nadia Haddad keeps the schedule.

## Harbor History Project

The Harbor History Project is digitizing the city's old newspapers. Grace Liu leads it, together
with the partner organization Harbor Historical Society. The scans are kept at the Central
branch.
